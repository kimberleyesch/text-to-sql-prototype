Title: Historical Revenue and Profit Calculation Rules

Content:
Historical calculations use the unit selling price and unit cost at the time an order was placed. Current product prices and current product costs are not used.

Gross revenue is calculated before discount:
Gross revenue = ordered quantity * historical unit selling price 

The discount is stored in discount_percent as a percentage between 0 and 100.
The discounted unit selling price is calculated as:
Discounted unit selling price = historical unit selling price * (1- discount percent / 100.0)

Net revenue is calculated as:
Net revenue = ordered quantity * discounted unit selling price 

Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is calculated as:
COGS = ordered quantity * historical unit cost

Gross profit is calculated as:
Gross profit = net revenue - COGS

Gross profit margin is calculated as:
Gross profit margin = gross profit / net revenue * 100

If net revenue is zero, the gross profit margin is undefined.

Average order value is calculated as:
Average order value = total net Revenue from completed orders / number of completed orders

Each completed order is counted once.

An order results in a loss if ist total gross profit is below zero.

The loss amount is calculated as:
Loss amount = absolute value of the order's negative total gross profit
