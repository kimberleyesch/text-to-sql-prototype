Titel: Customer Segment and VIP Definition

Content:
The automation segment consists of customers in following industries:
Industrial Automation, Robotics, Building Automation, Logistics Automation

A customer is classified a VIP-customer if at least one of the following conditions are met:
-	The customer has placed at least five completed orders.
-	The customer has generated more than 2000 € in net revenue from completed orders.

Net revenue is calculated using the historical unit selling price after discount.

Prioritized customers include all VIP-customers as well as customers with following customer IDs: 1, 42, 66, 100 and 404

The Growth Market Segment includes customers located in Germany, France, United States and China.
