Title: Customer and Order Status

Content:
A customer is classified as active if the value of is_active is 1.
A customer is classified as inactive if the value of is_active is 0.

An open order is an order with the status pending.
A successful purchase is an order with the status completed.

Unless another order status is explicitly stated, the terms "purchased products" and "sold products" refer to order items belonging to completed orders.

Cancelled orders are not considered successful purchases.
