Title: Historical Revenue and Profit Calculation Rules

Content:
Historical calculations use the selling price and unit costs at the time an order was placed. Current product prices and costs are not used.

Gross revenue is calculated by multiplying the ordered quantity by the historical unit selling price before discount.
Net revenue is calculated by applying the recorded discount to the historical unit selling price and multiplying the result by the ordered quantity.
Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) is calculated by multiplying the ordered quantity by the historical unit cost.
Gross profit is calculated as net revenue minus Cost of Goods Sold.
Gross profit margin is calculated as gross profit devided by net revenue, multiplied by 100.

If net revenue is zero, the gross profit margin is undefined.
